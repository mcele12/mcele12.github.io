Rooted in Kauai, Built on Aloha

Welcome to Garden Isle Event Rentals (GIER). We are a locally started and family-operated event company established in early 2022.

Here at GIER, we serve our clients with the authentic aloha spirit and professionalism that we were raised with right here on the beautiful island of Kauai.

Whether you are hosting an intimate family luau, a backyard birthday, a community gathering, or an elegant island wedding, we provide commercial-grade high-peak frame canopies and full event rentals tailored to your exact location.
